Veenashree Caterers Booking and Cancellation Policy

All catering orders must be confirmed at least 7 business days prior to the scheduled event date. A non-refundable deposit of 30% of the total estimated cost is required upon contract signing to secure your date. Guest counts must be finalized no later than 72 hours before the event; downward adjustments after this window cannot be accommodated due to perishable food procurement schedules.

Cancellations made between 7 days and 48 hours prior to the event will forfeit the initial 30% deposit, but no additional charges will apply. Cancellations made within 48 hours of the event will be billed at 80% of the total estimated invoice to cover incurred food prep and staffing commitments. In the event of extreme weather or official local emergency orders, clients may reschedule their booking within a 90-day window without additional penalty fees.
