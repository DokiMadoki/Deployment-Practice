**Booking & Deposit:**
*   Orders must be confirmed at least **7 business days** in advance.
*   A **non-refundable 30% deposit** is required to secure the booking.

**Guest Count:**
*   Final guest numbers are due **72 hours** before the event. Last-minute decreases cannot be accommodated.

**Cancellation Fees:**
*   **7 days to 48 hours before:** You lose the 30% deposit.
*   **Within 48 hours:** You are charged **80%** of the total cost.

**Special Circumstances:**
*   In cases of extreme weather or official emergencies, you can reschedule within 90 days without penalty.
